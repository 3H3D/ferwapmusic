About The Label

Ferwap Music is an independent record label, creative hub, and audio production house dedicated to empowering visionary music creators, audio engineers, and recording artists. Built on a foundation of creative independence and artistic integrity, we focus on long-term catalog development, audio engineering excellence, and strategic global distribution.

As a modern music entity, Ferwap Music bridges the gap between raw independent talent and global digital platforms. We provide complete end-to-end support for our roster—ranging from original composition, vocal tracking, and advanced mixing/mastering to copyright administration, digital asset protection, and multi-platform promotional campaigns.

Our mission is to foster meaningful collaborations among independent producers, songwriters, and vocalists while curating a distinct sound identity across diverse contemporary genres. By leveraging innovative production workflows and modern digital infrastructure, Ferwap Music ensures that every project reaches its full creative potential and finds its rightful audience worldwide.

A&R Submissions

Demo Submission

Ferwap music is constantly seeking innovative, passionate, and visionary talent to join our independent roster. Whether you are an emerging artist, vocalist, or music producer, we provide a collaborative space to develop your catalog and refine your sound.

Submission Guidelines:

  • Format: We only accept private streaming links (SoundCloud, Google Drive, or Dropbox). Please do not attach raw MP3/WAV files directly via email.
  • Track Selection: Send 2 to 3 of your best unreleased tracks. Make sure your audio links are set to accessible permissions.
  • Metadata & Info: Include your official artist name, a short biography, active social media links, and clear contact information.

What We Look For:

  • Originality and a strong artistic identity.
  • Solid songwriting, production potential, and high-quality arrangements.
  • Dedication to long-term catalog development and strategic collaboration.

Due to the high volume of submissions, our A&R team manually reviews every entry. If your music fits, we will reach out to you within 2–3 weeks.

Commercial Services

Music Licensing

Ferwap music manages an independent catalog of original recordings and compositions tailored for sync licensing across worldwide media platforms. We partner with content creators, film directors, game developers, advertising agencies, and production houses.

Our Licensing Services:

  • Synchronization Rights: Master and publishing clearance for film, television, documentaries, video games, and commercial advertisements.
  • Custom Audio Production: Original sound design, mix engineering, and bespoke track production customized to meet project-specific briefs.
  • Digital Media & Branded Content: Pre-cleared music assets for digital campaigns, social media content, and corporate videos.

How to Request a License:

  1. Project title and brief description (e.g., commercial, short film, game).
  2. Specific track title(s) requested.
  3. Media format and distribution territory.
  4. Term/Duration of the license and your estimated budget.
